Lesly M. Queen

M.A. in Theatre Studies, B.A. in Acting on Stage and Screen

Lesly M. Queen is an entertainment and creative arts professional with a strong specialty in theatre, working across performance, production, design, direction, education, and arts marketing. With a career rooted in both the creative and technical sides of live entertainment, Lesly brings a multidisciplinary and adaptable approach to storytelling and collaboration.

She holds a Master of Arts in Theatre Studies from the University of Central Florida and a Bachelor of Arts in Acting for Stage and Screen, with a minor in Secondary-Level Education, from Nova Southeastern University. Her academic training, paired with extensive hands-on experience, has shaped a well-rounded perspective on theatrical production, creative leadership, and audience engagement.

Lesly has worked in a wide range of creative environments throughout South Florida, including academic institutions, professional theatre companies, and live performance settings. Her experience spans acting, costume and make-up design, directing, stage management, and teaching artists of all ages. She has collaborated with organizations such as Inside Out Theatre, Thinking Cap Theatre, This Is Improv!, and City Theatre, contributing both onstage and behind the scenes in a variety of roles.

Currently, Lesly serves as a Theatre Professor at Nova Southeastern University and most recently worked as the Costume and Make-Up Designer for Cymbeline and RENT. In addition to her theatrical work, she continues to expand her creative reach through arts marketing and live-event engagement, supporting creative brands and artists at conventions and public-facing events.

Known for her approachable, collaborative nature and broad skill set, Lesly thrives in multidisciplinary environments and values adaptability, creative problem-solving, and meaningful collaboration. Whether designing, directing, performing, teaching, or supporting creative commerce, she is committed to crafting engaging experiences and building lasting artistic connections.

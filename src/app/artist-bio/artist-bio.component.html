Lesly M. Queen, M.A.

Performing Arts Educator | Multidisciplinary Art & Entertainment Professional

I am an entertainment and creative arts professional working at the intersection of theatre education, arts promotion, and content-driven marketing. With over a decade of experience across performance, production, design, and live events, I bring a multidisciplinary and people-centered approach to storytelling, audience engagement, and creative collaboration.

I currently serve as Adjunct Faculty in Theatre Studies, teaching undergraduate courses grounded in performance analysis, theatrical practice, and production processes. At the heart of my teaching is collaboration—encouraging ensemble building, teamwork, and thoughtful communication to help students grow into confident, adaptable creatives prepared for real-world artistic environments.

Alongside my academic work, I am a Creative Marketing & Sales Associate with LenLenCreates, where I focus on arts promotion, content marketing, and brand representation for original musical theatre–inspired artwork. My work includes marketing strategy, visual merchandising, live-event promotion, and direct audience engagement at conventions and markets, blending creative storytelling with meaningful community connection.

My professional theatre experience spans directing, costume and make-up design, and a variety of production roles. I have served as Costume & Make-Up Designer for RENT and Cymbeline, and directed 101 Dalmatians Jr., combining artistic vision with collaborative leadership and hands-on production work.

I hold a Master of Arts in Theatre Studies and a Bachelor of Arts in Theatre (Stage & Screen Acting), with a minor in Secondary-Level Education. I am deeply passionate about mentorship, inclusive storytelling, and creating spaces where artists and audiences feel seen, welcomed, and inspired.

If you’re interested in collaborating, developing creative work, or simply starting a conversation, I’d love to connect. Please feel free to reach out—I’m always excited to explore new projects and creative partnerships.
